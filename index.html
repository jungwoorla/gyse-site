How GYSE works

Logging takes seconds after your heaviest set. The rest happens on its own.

  1. 1Log your top set

    One heaviest set per exercise. GYSE remembers last time and suggests the next target to beat.

  2. 2Your index closes the day

    Each exercise feeds its sector, and the sectors blend into one line that began at 100. Miss a week and a market holiday keeps your run alive.

  3. 3Share it, follow friends

    Post your week as a story card. Claim a ticker of 1 to 8 letters, and friends follow your line on their watchlist.
