Questions.

Is GYSE free?

Yes. Logging, the index, following people and sharing are free. GYSE Plus is optional: it adds candle charts, moving averages, your own exercises and a third market holiday.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone, with iOS 18 or later. There is no Android, iPad or Apple Watch app.

When does it launch?

Soon. There is no date yet. Reserve a ticker and we'll email you once, when it's out.

What does reserving a ticker do?

It holds that ticker for your email address. We send one email with a link; open it within 48 hours to confirm. After launch, sign up in the app with the same email within 30 days and the ticker is yours. One ticker per email.

How is the index calculated?

Each exercise is measured by its estimated one-rep max, compared with your own first day. Sets of 3 to 10 reps count. Together your exercises move one index that starts at 100. How the index works

Who can see my training?

Anyone can find your ticker and see your name and photo, your index with its recent line, and how many people follow you and how many you follow. You choose a public or private profile when you sign up. With a private profile, only followers you approve see your exercises and sessions. Privacy Policy

Does it track running or cardio?

No. GYSE is for strength training.
