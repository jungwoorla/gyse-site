    GYSE

    1. Log your heaviest set.

      One top set per exercise is all it takes. GYSE remembers last time, fills the weight in for you and shows the next target to beat.

    2. Your index closes the day.

      Every exercise feeds its sector, and the sectors blend into one line that started at 100. Train every week and your run grows. Miss one and a market holiday covers it.

    3. Claim a ticker. See who's up.

      Pick 1–8 letters that are yours alone. Friends add you to their watchlist, and you can go head to head on any month.

    4. Post the close.

      Every session makes a story card and a transparent sticker of your top sets and their moves, sized for Instagram.
