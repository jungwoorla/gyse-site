Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

GYSE turns the top sets you log into a personal strength index. This page explains what the app stores, who can see it, and how you stay in control. The short version: your email is never shown to anyone, you choose whether your log is public or friends-only, and you can delete everything from inside the app.

What GYSE stores

  • Account: your email address and a password, or your Apple or Google account through Sign in with Apple or Continue with Google, used only to sign you in. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us. With Sign in with Apple, Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real email. With Google we receive your email address and name. The name Apple or Google shares only pre-fills your display name, and we never see your Apple or Google password.
  • Profile: your ticker (1–8 English letters, for example $JUNG), the display name you choose, whether your profile is public or private, and when you last changed your ticker.
  • Workouts: the exercise, weight, reps and day of each top set you log, any notes you add, and any exercises you create.
  • Settings: your unit (kg / lb), chosen exercises and allocations, and chart preferences.
  • Index summary: your current index value and up to 30 recent index points.
  • Friends and safety: friend requests, people you block, and reports you send.
  • Anonymous usage and crash data: see below.

Who can see what

Anyone signed in to GYSE can look you up by your exact ticker and see your ticker, display name and index (with its recent line).

Public profile (the default): everyone can also see your exercises and the sets you log (exercise, weight, reps and day).

Private profile: only people you have accepted as friends see your exercises and sets. Switch at any time in You → Private profile.

Never shown to anyone: your email address, your password and your set notes. Blocking someone hides your profile from them and removes any request between you.

Keeping GYSE safe

Tickers and names are checked against a list of reserved and offensive words. You can report any profile from its page; reports go privately to the GYSE team, who review them within 24 hours and may remove content or accounts that break the rules.

Anonymous usage and crash data

To fix bugs and understand which features help, the app sends a small number of usage events (for example "logged a set" or "opened the share card") and Apple's crash and hang reports to our own backend. They are tied to a random ID created on your phone, not to your account, and never include your email, ticker, name, notes or weights. Turn this off any time in You → Share anonymous usage data.

Where it lives

Your data is stored on your iPhone and synced to a hosted database (Supabase). Access is enforced on the server, row by row: you can change only your own records, and other people can read only what the rules above allow.

What we don't do

  • No third-party advertising, no ad identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of your data.
  • No access to contacts, photos or location. Sharing a card uses the standard iOS share sheet; the image goes only where you send it.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs. The app talks to one service: its own backend.

Deleting your data

Open You → Delete account. This removes your account, profile, workouts, custom exercises, settings, index summary, friendships and blocks from the server immediately and wipes the copy on your phone. There is no retention period and nothing to email us about — though you are welcome to.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date above changes and the app will point you here.

Contact

Questions about privacy or your data: support@gyse.app.
