GYSE turns the top sets you log into a personal strength index. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, where it is stored and how you stay in control. 1. Who we are

GYSE (the app and gyse.app) is operated by Jungwoo Kim, an independent developer in the Republic of Korea ("GYSE", "we", "us"). We decide how your personal information is used, so we are its controller (in Korean law, the personal information controller).

Privacy officer: Jungwoo Kim, support@gyse.app. Contact this address for any privacy question or request.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and password, or the account details Apple or Google share when you use Sign in with Apple or Continue with Google (an email address, which Apple may replace with a private relay address, and optionally your name). Passwords are stored as one-way hashes by our authentication provider. We never see your Apple or Google password.
  • Profile: your ticker (1–8 English letters), the display name you choose, whether your profile is public or private, and when you last changed your ticker.
  • Training log: for each top set, the exercise, weight, reps and day, any note you add, and the custom exercises you create.
  • Settings: units (kg or lb), chosen exercises, sector allocation and chart preferences.
  • Social and safety: friend requests and friendships, people you block, and reports you send (the reason, any details you type and the account reported).
  • Messages to us: anything you send to support@gyse.app.

Information created as you use GYSE

  • Index summary: your current index value and up to 30 recent index points, calculated from your log so friends and the watchlist can show your line.
  • Usage and crash data: a small set of app events (for example "logged a set", "opened the share card" or "viewed the paywall") and the crash, hang and performance reports Apple provides through MetricKit, together with the app version, iOS version and device model. These are tied to a random ID created on your iPhone, not to your account, and never include your email, ticker, name, notes or weights. You can turn this off at any time (see section 8).
  • Purchases: if you buy GYSE Plus, Apple tells the app which plan is active. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment card details.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider records IP addresses and request metadata in short-lived server logs, and uses IP addresses to limit abuse such as repeated sign-in attempts.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, location, advertising identifier, or health records, and GYSE does not read from or write to Apple Health. The weights and reps you log are training records, not medical information.

4. Who can see what

  • Everyone using GYSE can find you by ticker and see your ticker, display name and index with its recent line.
  • Public profile (the default): anyone using GYSE can also see your exercises and sessions (exercise, weight, reps and day).
  • Private profile: only people you accept as friends see your exercises and sessions. Switch at any time in You → Private profile.
  • Never shown to anyone: your email address, password, set notes, the people you block and the reports you send.
  • Share cards and stickers are created on your iPhone and go only where you send them with the iOS share sheet.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, log, settings, index summary and social data: until you delete your account. Deleting it removes them from our database immediately. Copies in our hosting provider's backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Reports: up to 12 months after review, so we can deal with repeated abuse, then deleted. Reports about you are deleted with your account; reports you sent stay until then, no longer linked to you.
  • Usage and crash data: up to 24 months, then deleted. Because it is not linked to your account, it is not removed when you delete your account.
  • Server logs and rate-limit records: kept by our hosting provider for a short period, normally no more than 90 days.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to help you, and no longer than 3 years.

When a retention period ends we delete the data from our database, which cannot be restored afterwards. We keep data longer only where the law requires it, and then only for that purpose.

8. Your rights and choices

  • See and correct: your profile, log and settings are shown in the app, and you can edit them there.
  • Take a copy: You → Your data → Export as CSV exports every top set you have logged.
  • Delete: You → Delete account deletes your account and its data. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke GYSE's access to your Apple ID.
  • Limit who sees your training: turn on Private profile.
  • Turn off usage data: You → Share anonymous usage data.
  • Ask us: email support@gyse.app to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or to object to processing. We may ask you to confirm the request from your account email. We reply within 10 days for requests under Korean law and within one month otherwise.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Korea: the Personal Information Protection Commission (privacy.go.kr, call 182) or the KISA Privacy Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, call 118). In the EU or UK, the authority where you live (in the UK, the ICO). If you live in California, you have the rights to know, delete and correct, and we will not treat you differently for using them. We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

9. How we protect it

  • All connections between the app, the website and our servers are encrypted (TLS).
  • The database enforces access rules on every row: you can change only your own records, and others can read only what section 4 allows. Sensitive operations run through restricted database roles.
  • Your session is stored in the iPhone Keychain. Passwords are hashed and never stored in readable form.
  • Sign-in, lookups, reports and requests are rate-limited to slow down abuse.
  • Only the operator of GYSE can access production data, and only to run and support the service.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

10. Age limits

GYSE is not intended for children. You must be at least 14 to create an account, and in the EU, EEA or UK you must also be old enough to consent to data processing in your country, or have a parent's consent where the law allows. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under these ages, we will delete it. Parents can contact us at support@gyse.app.

11. This website

gyse.app uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads its fonts from gyse.app itself. GitHub, which hosts the site, receives your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and protect the service.
