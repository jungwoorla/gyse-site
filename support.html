Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: email support@gyse.app with your ticker and iOS version. We aim to reply within two days.

How the index works

Your index starts at 100 on the first day you log a qualifying set, and moves with the weighted average of your exercises' day-to-day changes, like a stock index made of your lifts.

  • Each exercise's level is its estimated one-rep max (Epley: weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30)) compared with its own first day.
  • Only sets of 3–10 reps count. Heavier singles and high-rep sets are saved but don't move the line.
  • Sectors (Chest, Back, Legs, Shoulders, Arms) carry the share you set in You → Exercises; exercises inside a sector share it equally.
  • An exercise joins the index the day after its first set, so adding a new lift never jolts your line.

Common questions

Why didn't my index move?

The set was outside 3–10 reps, it was the first set for that exercise (that day sets its starting point), or the exercise isn't one of your chosen exercises.

I logged the wrong number.

Open the exercise from the Index screen and tap the set under History to edit or delete it. Your index recalculates straight away.

Can I change my ticker?

Yes, once every 7 days in You → Ticker. Tickers are 1–8 English letters and must be unique. Your display name can be in any language.

Who can see my training?

Anyone can find you by ticker and see your name and index. With a public profile they also see your exercises and sessions; with Private profile on, only friends do. Your email and notes are never shown. More in the Privacy Policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything is saved on your iPhone first and synced when you're back online.

Can I bring my history from another app?

You → Import from Strong or Hevy reads their CSV export and keeps your best set per exercise per day. Export as CSV gives you a copy of everything.

Reporting and blocking

Open someone's profile and tap ⋯ to Report or Block them. Reports are private, and we review every one within 24 hours. Blocking works both ways: neither of you can find or see the other. For anything urgent, email support@gyse.app with the ticker involved.

GYSE Plus

  • Cancel: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → GYSE. Plus stays on until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it.
  • Restore on a new iPhone: open the Plus screen and tap Restore purchases with the same Apple ID.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your account

You → Delete account removes your account, every set you've logged and your friendships from our servers and your iPhone. It can't be undone, so export first if you want a copy. Signed in with Apple? We also revoke GYSE's access to your Apple ID.
