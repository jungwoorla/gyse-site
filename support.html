How the index works

Your index starts at 100 on the first day you log a qualifying set, and moves with the weighted average of your exercises' day-to-day returns — like a stock index made of your lifts.

  • Each exercise's level is its estimated 1RM (Epley: weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30)) relative to its own first day.
  • Only sets of 3–10 reps count. Heavier singles and high-rep sets are saved but don't move the line.
  • Sectors (Chest, Back, Legs, Shoulders, Arms) carry the allocation you set in You → Exercises; exercises inside a sector share it equally.
  • An exercise joins the index the day after its first set, so adding a new lift never jolts your line.

Common questions

Why didn't my index move?

Either the set was outside 3–10 reps, it was the very first set for that exercise (that day sets its baseline), or the exercise isn't one of your chosen exercises.

Can I change my ticker?

Yes — once every 7 days, in You → Ticker. Tickers are 1–8 English letters (A–Z) and must be unique. Your display name can be in any language.

How do friends see me?

Anyone can find you by your exact ticker and see your name and index. With a public profile they also see your exercises and sets; with Private profile on (in You), only friends do. Your email is never shown. To remove, block or report someone, open their profile and tap ⋯.

I logged the wrong number.

Open the exercise from the Index screen and tap the set in History to edit or delete it. Your index recalculates instantly.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything is saved on your phone first and synced when you're back online. The cloud icon on the Index screen shows what's waiting.

Deleting my account

You → Delete account removes everything, on the server and on your phone, immediately.
